HUMAN isn't an enterprise directory with AI added to it.

It's a personal AI-powered human network that belongs to each individual.

Your organisation provides access to its private community. Your personal HUMAN identity can continue with you beyond that community, subject to other people's privacy and consent.

It can help you:

  • Discover people you actually want to meet.
  • Manage messages, calls, and conversations.
  • Remember important relationships, shared moments, and commitments.
  • Grow your network through relevant, consented introductions.
  • Coordinate plans and complete authorised tasks.
  • Create a personal AI twin that can represent you within permissions you control.
  • Explore human connections through emerging wearable and spatial interfaces.
