HHUMAN

01. What is HUMAN?

HUMAN is Uber for human connections and relationships. An AI-powered network making it effortless to find, meet, and connect with the right people, anywhere in the world.

But HUMAN goes beyond connecting people. It's also your personal AI, designed to take care of your communications, relationships, and everyday life—from answering messages and handling calls to completing tasks and making things happen on your behalf.

HUMAN is a product of HUTE, the company building the intelligence and technology behind this new way of living and connecting.

02. What is HUTE, and how is it related to HUMAN?

HUTE is the company. HUMAN is the product.

HUTE is the technology company building HUMAN, with a mission to make artificial intelligence a natural part of everyday human life.

HUTE develops the underlying AI systems, intelligent agents, relationship technology, and infrastructure that power HUMAN.

HUMAN is how people experience that technology: one connected experience for finding people, building relationships, communicating, and getting things done.

Put simply, HUTE builds the intelligence. HUMAN brings it into your life.

03. What does "Uber for people" actually mean?

Think about how easy it is to get a ride. You open Uber, tell it where you want to go, and a driver comes to you.

HUMAN brings that simplicity to meeting people.

Whether you're looking for a friend, a date, someone to share an experience with, or someone who can help, HUMAN is being built to connect you with the right willing person.

The ambition is simple: finding the right human should be as effortless as finding a ride.

04. Why does the world need HUMAN?

We can reach billions of people online, yet finding the right person at the right moment is still unnecessarily difficult.

At the same time, we spend enormous amounts of our lives managing messages, emails, calls, schedules, applications, and everyday responsibilities.

HUMAN exists to change both.

We believe meaningful human connection should be one of the easiest things in the world, and technology should give us more time to experience life rather than consume it.

05. Is HUMAN a social network?

Not in the traditional sense.

Social networks are built around feeds, followers, and content. HUMAN is built around people, connections, and real experiences.

The goal isn't to keep you scrolling. It's to bring the right people into your life.

06. Is HUMAN a dating app?

It can help you find someone to date, but dating is only one small part of HUMAN.

Make friends, discover people nearby, find activity partners, meet collaborators, book experiences, or simply have a meaningful conversation.

HUMAN is for every kind of human connection.

07. How does HUMAN find the right people for me?

Just tell HUMAN what you're looking for, in your own words.

Its AI is designed to understand your interests, intentions, preferences, location, and availability to find compatible people who are open to connecting.

Instead of searching through endless profiles, you describe the human connection you're looking for.

08. Can I meet people near me?

Absolutely—that's central to the vision.

HUMAN is being designed around real-world discovery, including a live map that helps you find people nearby who have chosen to be discoverable.

Coffee, conversations, activities, and spontaneous connections could be just around the corner.

09. Can I meet someone right now?

That's the idea behind on-demand human connection.

HUMAN aims to help you find people who are available and interested in connecting now.

A successful match still depends on the right person being available and both people wanting to connect.

10. Can I connect with people anywhere in the world?

That's what we're building toward.

Your next meaningful connection shouldn't be limited by where you live.

HUMAN aims to make discovering and meeting people across cities, countries, and continents feel effortless.

11. Do both people have to agree before connecting?

Yes.

Every meaningful connection begins with mutual choice.

HUMAN can discover, recommend, and propose introductions, but nobody should be forced into a conversation, meeting, or relationship.

You remain in control of whom you connect with.

12. Can HUMAN arrange meetings for me?

That's part of the vision.

Instead of endless messages trying to find a time and place, HUMAN will be able to help coordinate availability, suggest meeting places, handle scheduling, and guide you to your agreed destination.

From finding someone to actually meeting them, the experience is designed to feel seamless.

13. Can I book or pay someone through HUMAN?

HUMAN is building toward a world where you can discover, connect with, and book people offering services, expertise, or experiences.

The vision includes secure payments and a seamless experience from discovery to completion.

Whether you're arranging an experience or booking someone's time, HUMAN aims to make the process effortless and transparent.

14. What makes HUMAN different from Instagram, Tinder, or LinkedIn?

Those platforms help you discover profiles, content, or specific kinds of connections.

HUMAN wants to help you find the right person for almost any human need—and make the connection actually happen.

But HUMAN also goes beyond discovery.

Its personal AI is designed to handle communication, maintain relationships, and complete everyday tasks across the services you use.

Less browsing. Less managing. More living.

15. Does HUMAN use AI?

AI is at the heart of HUMAN.

It understands what you need, helps find the right people, coordinates introductions, manages relationships, and can complete everyday tasks on your behalf.

HUTE is building the underlying AI technology to make these experiences possible.

The intelligence works in the background. The experience stays human.

16. What is my personal AI twin?

Imagine an AI version of you that understands your personality, interests, goals, and preferences.

Your HUMAN twin can represent you in authorised interactions, communicate on your behalf, and help you stay connected—even when you're busy living your life.

It's not meant to replace you. It's meant to extend what you can do.

17. Can my AI twin talk to someone else's AI twin?

That's one of HUMAN's most ambitious ideas.

Your AI twin could communicate with another person's AI twin to explore compatibility, coordinate availability, and propose an introduction.

Imagine two personal AIs handling the awkward logistics of getting to know someone, so the humans can focus on the actual connection.

The decision to connect always belongs to the people involved.

18. Can HUMAN handle my messages, emails, and calls?

That's a core part of what we're building.

HUMAN is designed to work across your authorised communication channels, helping you respond, coordinate, follow up, and take action without constantly switching between applications.

The long-term goal is much bigger than an AI that drafts messages.

Imagine incoming emails being handled, conversations being followed up, appointments being arranged, and requests being completed—all within permissions you control.

Your digital life should run without requiring your constant attention.

19. Can HUMAN actually complete tasks for me?

Yes—that's one of HUMAN's defining ambitions.

Not just answering questions or setting reminders, but actually getting things done.

HUMAN is being designed to understand requests from messages, calls, emails, notifications, and everyday interactions, then complete authorised tasks across your connected services.

Someone asks you to arrange dinner? HUMAN could find a suitable restaurant, coordinate everyone's availability, and arrange the reservation.

An email requires action? HUMAN could understand the request and handle the necessary steps.

The goal is a world where your AI doesn't simply tell you what needs to happen.

It makes things happen.

20. Will HUMAN manage my relationships automatically?

That's the vision.

HUMAN will help remember important details, maintain contact, follow up on conversations, and recognise when someone matters to you.

Imagine never forgetting an important promise, losing touch with someone meaningful, or overlooking a relationship because life got busy.

Relationships deserve attention. Managing them shouldn't feel like another job.

21. Can HUMAN introduce me to people I didn't know I needed?

That's one of the most exciting possibilities.

The right person can change your life before you even realise you need to meet them.

HUMAN aims to discover meaningful connections proactively, based on your goals, interests, preferences, and openness to meeting new people.

Rather than requiring you to search for every connection, HUMAN could help the right connections find you.

22. Will HUMAN work with ChatGPT, X, and Meta?

We envision HUMAN extending beyond its own experience and connecting with AI assistants, social platforms, and communication services wherever supported integrations and permissions allow.

Imagine asking an AI assistant to find someone—and HUMAN helping turn that request into a real connection.

HUTE's ambition is to make HUMAN's network accessible across the digital experiences people already use.

These integrations are part of our vision, not a claim of existing partnerships with OpenAI, X, Meta, or their respective products.

23. Will HUMAN work with smart glasses?

That's where we see human connection heading.

As computers move from pockets to faces, HUMAN aims to make discovering people, receiving introductions, and navigating real-world meetings possible through compatible AI and AR glasses.

Imagine moving through the world with a personal AI that can help you discover opportunities to connect with people around you, with appropriate privacy protections and consent.

The technology should fit into your life, not interrupt it.

24. What about virtual reality?

Sometimes the right person lives thousands of kilometres away.

HUMAN envisions shared virtual spaces where people can meet, talk, and spend time together regardless of distance.

Whether through a phone, smart glasses, or a virtual environment, the ambition remains the same.

Real connections don't always have to begin in the same physical room.

25. Do I need smart glasses or a VR headset to use HUMAN?

No.

HUMAN is designed to begin with the devices you already use.

Smart glasses, AR, and VR are future extensions of the experience—not requirements for human connection.

You shouldn't need to buy new hardware to find the right people or benefit from personal AI.

26. Can people see my exact location?

HUMAN is designed to give you control over your visibility.

Nearby discovery can use approximate location, while more precise location sharing should happen only with appropriate permission.

Being discoverable should never mean surrendering your privacy.

27. Is my personal information safe?

Privacy and control are fundamental to the product we're building.

HUTE's goal is to develop HUMAN with clear choices about what your AI knows, what it shares, who can discover you, and which actions it can take.

Sensitive information should never be exposed simply to make a match.

Our aim is to make meaningful connections possible without compromising personal boundaries.

28. Can HUMAN do things without constantly asking me?

That's exactly the kind of experience we're designing.

HUMAN is being built to handle routine activities automatically within permissions you define, while requesting approval for sensitive, consequential, or irreversible actions.

Imagine your AI taking care of routine messages, coordination, follow-ups, and everyday responsibilities without making you supervise every step.

You define the boundaries. HUMAN handles what falls within them.

The goal is less micromanagement without giving up control.

29. Will HUMAN replace real human interaction?

Quite the opposite.

HUMAN exists because we believe real human interaction matters more than ever.

AI should handle the friction of finding, organising, and maintaining connections—not replace the people you connect with.

We want technology to become less of a destination and more of an invisible force that helps you live.

More people. More experiences. More life.

30. Will HUMAN be free?

We want meaningful human connection to be accessible to everyone.

The intended model includes accessible core connections alongside optional premium AI capabilities and paid services or experiences.

Specific pricing will be announced as the product develops.

31. What is HUMAN ultimately trying to build?

A world where finding the right human is as effortless as getting an Uber.

Where your personal AI understands your life, handles your communications, maintains your relationships, and completes your everyday tasks.

Where connecting with someone across the street or across the planet feels natural.

Where technology brings people closer together instead of keeping them apart.

This is the future HUTE is building through HUMAN.

Not another place to spend your time.

A way to get more of your life back.

The internet connected our devices. AI connected us to intelligence. HUMAN is here to connect us to each other.
